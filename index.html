    About Tripped

    Every power plant has problems that never make it into a report: spares that take months, approvals that stall, knowledge that leaves when people retire. Tripped puts them on one map, so we can see which ones are everywhere.

    • No account, no name, no ads, no tracking cookies. Your IP address isn't saved with anything you post.
    • Lamps sit near your country, scattered on purpose, never on a real plant.
    • Lamps grow brighter as more people tap "me too". New ones pulse for 24 hours.
    • Posts naming companies or people, or with links or phone numbers, are held for review. Three reports take a post down until it's checked.
    • When someone starts fixing a problem, its lamp turns green.
    • Lamps marked "Sample" are examples to get things started.

    Built by a power plant engineer as a side project. Feedback is welcome.
